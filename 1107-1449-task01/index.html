"Mix-Mix" — the beloved Filipino shaved ice dessert

What is Halo-Halo?

HAH-loh HAH-loh

Halo-halo (from Tagalog halo, meaning "mix") is the Philippines' iconic summer dessert — a colorful parfait of shaved ice, sweet fruits, jellies, beans, and custard, crowned with ice cream and drizzled with evaporated milk. It's the delicious answer to tropical heat: no two spoonfuls are ever the same, because half the fun is mixing everything together in the tall glass!

A Little Culture

Halo-halo traces its roots to pre-war Japanese influence (from the shaved-ice treat kakigōri) blended with local Filipino sweets. Today, every region — and every household — has its own version. In summer, halo-halo stands pop up on street corners across the archipelago, and rivals like Razon's and Chowking keep the debate alive: simple and smooth, or loaded to the brim?
